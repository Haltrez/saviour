01 / The problem

The trenches are rigged before the first candle.

A

Bundled launches

Deployers fund a ring of wallets and buy their own coin in the launch block. Bundling tools make it one click. The chart looks organic, but the supply is already owned, and you are the exit liquidity.

B

Vamp launches

A coin gets traction and within minutes a clone appears with the same name, the same image and a better-funded bot behind it. The original community gets split and drained.

C

Ticker races

News breaks and a dozen deployers race to mint the same ticker. Most of them are bots. Buyers can't tell which coin is real, and most of them pick the wrong one.

02 / The protocol

Rules in the program, not in a tweet.

Each guarantee is meant to be enforced by the Saviour program itself, so anyone can verify it onchain and no team can quietly turn it off.

S-01

0.5 SOL deploy, bought & burned

Every launch costs 0.5 SOL. The program swaps the full amount into $SAVIOUR and burns it in the same transaction. Spam launches cost real money, and the activity flows to holders as supply reduction.

S-02

30-minute ticker lock

Once a ticker deploys, nobody can deploy it again for 30 minutes. Matching is done on a normalised form, so SAV1OUR, $saviour and Cyrillic look-alikes all count as the same ticker.

S-03

Fair Start

The first 45 seconds of every coin run as a single-price batch auction. Every buyer in the window pays the same price, so there's no first-block edge.

S-04

Bundle Score

A funding-graph scan of early buyers and wallets funded by the dev, shown as a badge on every coin before you buy.

S-05

Origin badge

News coins attach their source link. The first deploy of that source is marked Origin, and later copies are labelled as clones that link back to it.

S-06

Vamp Shield

A perceptual-hash match on the image and similarity checks on the name block near-identical clones while the original is still trending.

S-07

Declared dev buy

The dev buy is capped, public and shown on the coin page. Dev sells are pushed to the live feed as they happen.

S-08

Snipe tax → burn

After Fair Start, a decaying tax applies to the first minutes of trading. What it collects buys and burns $SAVIOUR.

    07 / FAQ

    Questions from the trenches.

    Why launch on pump.fun if you're competing with it?

    Because that's where the trenches are. Launching $SAVIOUR there with a fair, unbundled start shows the standard we're setting, in front of the users we want to win over.

    Where does the 0.5 SOL go?

    All of it goes to buying $SAVIOUR on the open market, and the tokens are burned in the same transaction. The team takes none of the deploy fee.

    Can't someone just use a slightly different ticker?

    Tickers are normalised before the lock check: case, symbols, look-alike characters such as 0/O and 1/I, and Cyrillic homoglyphs. Near-misses within one character are flagged on the coin page. A ticker that's genuinely different is allowed through, which is the point.

    Doesn't the lock let one person squat a ticker?

    Squatting costs 0.5 SOL every 30 minutes, and all of it is burned. Squatters end up funding the burn. Origin badges also let the real news coin stand out.

    Is the launchpad live?

    Not yet. The roadmap above shows where we are. The token comes first, then the devnet registry, then mainnet. We'll ship in public and won't announce dates we can't meet.
