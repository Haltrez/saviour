    The protocol

    How Saviour saves you

    Each rule is meant to be enforced by the Saviour program itself, so anyone can check it onchain and nobody can quietly switch it off.

    S-01

    0.5 SOL deploy, bought & burned

    The program swaps the whole deploy fee into $SAVIOUR and burns it. Spam costs money, and every launch shrinks supply.

    S-02

    30-minute ticker lock

    Nobody can deploy the same ticker again for 30 minutes. SAV1OUR, $saviour and Cyrillic look-alikes all count as one.

    S-03

    Fair Start

    The first 45 seconds are a single-price batch. Everyone in the window pays the same price.

    S-04

    Bundle Score

    A funding-graph scan of early buyers, shown as a badge on every coin.

    S-05

    Origin badge

    News coins attach their source. The first deploy is marked Origin, and later ones are labelled clones.

    S-06

    Vamp Shield

    Image and name matching block near-identical copies of trending coins.

    S-07

    Declared dev buy

    Capped and public. Dev sells appear in the live feed as they happen.

    S-08

    Snipe tax → burn

    A fee that starts high and falls over the first minutes. The extra buys and burns $SAVIOUR.

    FAQ

    Questions from the trenches

    Why launch on pump.fun if you're competing with it?

    Because that's where the trenches are. A clean, unbundled launch there shows the standard we're setting, in front of the users we want to win over.

    Where does the 0.5 SOL go?

    All of it buys $SAVIOUR on the open market, and those tokens are burned. The team takes none of the deploy fee.

    Can't someone just use a slightly different ticker?

    Tickers are normalised before the lock check, which covers case, symbols, 0/O and 1/I swaps and Cyrillic look-alikes. Tickers one character away are flagged on the coin page.

    Doesn't the lock let one person squat a ticker?

    Squatting costs 0.5 SOL every 30 minutes, and all of it is burned. Squatters end up funding the burn.

    Is the launchpad live?

    Not yet. The token comes first, then the devnet registry, then mainnet. We'll build in public and won't announce dates we can't meet.
